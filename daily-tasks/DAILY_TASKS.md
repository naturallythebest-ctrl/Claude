# Enviro Pest LLC — Daily Operations Checklist

A daily task list for each team, laid out by time of day. Built around the
tools we already use: **Zoho CRM** (Leads, Deals, My Jobs, Tickets, Renewals,
Inactive Customers, Truck Orders, One Time, Calls, Tasks, Customer Invoice) and
**QuickBooks** (invoicing, payments, A/R).

> Times are suggestions. Adjust them to match your actual office hours and routes.

**Roles:** 🟦 Office / Customer Service · 🟩 Sales · 🟧 Technicians · 🟪 Billing · ⬛ Manager

---

## 1. Opening (7:00 – 8:00 AM)

| ✔ | Task | Who | Where |
|---|------|-----|-------|
| ☐ | Check voicemail, email, and SalesInbox. Flag anything urgent. | Office | Zoho SalesInbox |
| ☐ | Review today's schedule and routes in **My Jobs**. Fill any gaps. | Office | Zoho My Jobs |
| ☐ | Confirm today's appointments with customers (call or text). | Office | Zoho Calls |
| ☐ | Vehicle walk-around: tires, lights, fluids, sprayer equipment. | Technicians | Truck |
| ☐ | Check chemical and bait stock, PPE, labels, and SDS binder on the truck. | Technicians | Truck |
| ☐ | Review your route and job notes before leaving. | Technicians | Zoho My Jobs |
| ☐ | **7:30 team huddle (10 min):** today's jobs, callbacks, priorities, safety reminder. | Manager | In person |

## 2. Morning (8:00 AM – 12:00 PM)

| ✔ | Task | Who | Where |
|---|------|-----|-------|
| ☐ | Respond to every new lead within 1 hour. | Sales | Zoho Leads |
| ☐ | Follow up on open quotes and move each deal to its correct stage. | Sales | Zoho Deals |
| ☐ | Schedule new and one-time jobs; handle reschedules. | Office | Zoho One Time / My Jobs |
| ☐ | Complete each service. Note pest activity and conducive conditions, and take photos. | Technicians | Job site |
| ☐ | Record chemical application details for every stop: product, EPA reg #, amount, target pest, area treated. | Technicians | Service record |
| ☐ | Pass add-on service needs or referrals to the office as new leads. | Technicians | Zoho Leads |

## 3. Midday (12:00 – 2:00 PM)

| ✔ | Task | Who | Where |
|---|------|-----|-------|
| ☐ | Invoice yesterday's and this morning's completed jobs. | Billing | QuickBooks |
| ☐ | Attach invoice copies to the customer record. | Billing | Zoho Customer Invoice |
| ☐ | Check A/R aging. Send reminders on anything 30+ days past due. | Billing | QuickBooks |
| ☐ | Contact customers whose renewals are due in the next 30 days. | Office | Zoho Renewals |
| ☐ | Work the win-back list (skip anyone on **Inactive Never Send**). | Sales | Zoho Inactive Customers |

## 4. Afternoon (2:00 – 4:00 PM)

| ✔ | Task | Who | Where |
|---|------|-----|-------|
| ☐ | Resolve open tickets and complaints. Book re-services the same day. | Office | Zoho Tickets |
| ☐ | Process supply and truck orders submitted by technicians. | Manager | Zoho Truck Orders |
| ☐ | Log every call and outcome in the CRM. | Sales | Zoho Calls |
| ☐ | Check in with technicians on route progress and running-late jobs. | Office | Phone |

## 5. Close of Day (4:00 – 5:30 PM)

| ✔ | Task | Who | Where |
|---|------|-----|-------|
| ☐ | Mark all jobs complete with notes, photos, and chemical usage. | Technicians | Zoho My Jobs |
| ☐ | Submit truck and supply order requests for tomorrow. | Technicians | Zoho Truck Orders |
| ☐ | Lock up chemicals, clean equipment, and secure the vehicle. | Technicians | Truck |
| ☐ | Confirm tomorrow's schedule and send appointment reminders. | Office | Zoho My Jobs |
| ☐ | Close out or reassign today's open CRM tasks. | Office | Zoho Tasks |
| ☐ | Record payments received and prepare the deposit. | Billing | QuickBooks |
| ☐ | **Daily review:** jobs done vs. scheduled, new leads, deals won, open tickets, payments in. | Manager | Zoho Reports / QuickBooks |

---

## Every Friday (add-on)

- ☐ Review the week's numbers: revenue, jobs completed, close rate, callbacks. (Manager)
- ☐ Check chemical inventory against next week's schedule. (Manager)
- ☐ Clean up duplicate or stale leads and deals. (Sales)
- ☐ Run an A/R aging summary and plan collections. (Billing)
